## Interpretation boundary

The replication supports the paper's narrower claim: the layers expose and contain different failure modes, but no layer proves the user's original intent. Model proposal, protocol validity, execution eligibility, subject authorization, confirmation, and database fact ownership remain separate qualifications. Natural language may still be available to a non-authoritative presentation component; the evidence does not support a claim of total downstream language isolation.

The tested route is authority minimization in two existing Chinese single-node, read-only assistants. Bounded delegation and responsibility separation remain design directions. Create, update, delete, parallel dependency, distributed consistency, microservices, lifecycle races, payment, and compensation are presented only as reasons the broader problem grows; they are not findings of this experiment.
